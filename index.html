Know Before We Go

Common questions before your first pickup.

Do I need to be home?

No. Leave your laundry bag at your designated pickup location and we’ll notify you when it has been picked up.

Can I request fragrance-free detergent?

Yes. We can follow your preferred detergent, drying, and folding instructions whenever possible.

How fast will I get my laundry back?

Most orders are returned within 24 hours when available.

Do you serve Jacksonville?

Not at this time. We proudly serve Amelia Island, Fernandina Beach, and Yulee.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, card, Venmo, Cash App, and Zelle.
